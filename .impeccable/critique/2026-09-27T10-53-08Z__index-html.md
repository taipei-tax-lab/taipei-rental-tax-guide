## Design-specificity and strengths

The service is visibly grounded in Taipei tax work through its official identity, locally named owner scenarios, three tax categories, official filing links, and public-service tone. The owner/tenant distinction is clear. Scenario-first plan names help owners recognize their situation. Plan detail pages expose complete conditions, tax information, next steps, and official links. The quick guide offers progress, back/reset, retained answers, and a clear non-eligibility disclaimer.

## Highest-priority findings

1. **[P1] Conditions are visually separated from the benefit figures.** Plan tax numbers can attract attention before users see the shared caution or detailed conditions. Keep the relevant condition adjacent to each plan summary.
2. **[P2] The owner area presents four plans and a guide as five peer paths.** Preserve the guide, but clearly frame it as help for people who are unsure.
3. **[P2] A fixed illustration competes with long-form reading.** Keep the residential art at the opening and give tax, FAQ, and filing information a quiet reading surface.
4. **[P2] Four equal-width summary cards make the first scan dense.** Reorganize the summary so situation, benefit, and detail action can be compared without four narrow columns.

## Rethink direction

Make owner and tenant the two primary service routes; keep the assistant and income-standard filing route available as secondary tools. In the owner path, offer optional decision support before a single-column list of four scenario rows. Place tax benefit and related information in the same row, preserve the complete detail routes and comparison, and localize illustration to the hero. Treat tenant services, FAQs, and official resources as reading-oriented lists with comfortable spacing. On narrow screens, use a single-column reading path.

## Deliberately preserved

All four plans and their order, policy/tax text and values, guide logic, complete comparison, FAQ answers, official destinations, no-JavaScript plan information, skip/focus semantics, Messenger entry/configuration, and footer source-check date. No policy/content facts, CX/GCP settings, external dependencies, or new assets are part of this direction.
